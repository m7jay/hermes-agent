Hermes Agent

A personal AI agent that works with your Google account on your behalf.

What it does

Hermes Agent is a private, single-user automation assistant. With your explicit authorization it can read and search your Gmail, manage calendar events, access files in Drive, and read or edit Google Sheets and Docs — only when you ask it to.

Who it is for

This application is used by a single individual, its owner, on their own Google account. It is not a public service, is not distributed, and has no other users.

How it connects

Access is granted through Google OAuth 2.0. Credentials are stored locally on the owner's machine, never transmitted to a third party, and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
